Challenge

Story

—I felt it again—something off—but my feet just dragged me straight into the Chief’s office. Unlike the first task, though, I genuinely had no clue what the second one was.

The Chief didn’t even bother looking up from the pile of papers on his desk. He just grunted one word:

—Malware.

—…

I answered that with a big fat nothing.

Task

There is something rogue running on this machine.
What is the malware executable file name?

Tools
  • Volatility3
TLDR

Scan the dump with findevil, the suspiciously named .exe is the flag.

Answer:

epxlorer.exe

Solution

FindEvil

There is an infinite amount of ways to find out what executable is the malware but the simplest way to get some leads is the MemProcFS function findevil. What it effectively does is it checks all the running binaries in the dump for common signs of malware. You can read about what exactly it detects and how it does that, as well as how to use it here MemProcFS/wiki/FS_FindEvil . So lets see what it dredges up.

First you need to enable the forensic tools. You can read more about them here MemProcFS/wiki/FS_Forensic.

echo "4" > forensic/forensic_enable.txt

We have to wait for it to finish and then we can read the output of findevil.

cat forensic/findevil/findevil.txt | less
